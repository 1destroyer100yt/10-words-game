# Menu art generation

- Tool: built-in ImageGen (not CLI/API fallback).
- Final project asset: `Assets/Resources/Menu/menu-art.png`.
- Original: `<a Codex generated image>`.
- Integration: MenuScreen loads the sprite from Resources/Menu/menu-art; all controls are actual UI layered over the illustration.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background illustration for a Unity pixel-art stealth horror game's Start/Quit menu. Create one finished wide 16:9 image, 1536x864 if possible. Scene: a sinister horned devil looming over an abandoned architectural labyrinth, cupping a small faceted stolen red jewel between long clawed hands. The jewel is the focal point, framed by curved horns and sharp angular fingers. Dark corridors of a top-down floorplan extend towards the lower corners. Composition: devil and jewel in the TOP HALF; the entire lower central half must be almost pure black negative space for real interactive UI buttons overlaid later. Confine maze detail mainly to outer left and right edges, letting it disappear into black. Mood: ominous, restrained, striking indie horror title art. Medium: deliberate crisp pixel art with geometric silhouettes and sparse pixel dithering, no smooth painterly gradients, no photographic texture. STRICT three-color palette ONLY: pure black #000000 background, dark grey #464646 architecture and body details, vivid red #ed1c24 jewel and eyes and sparse highlights. No white or additional colors. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no UI buttons baked into image. Highly legible jewel silhouette and separated fingers. This is atmospheric game menu art, not a mockup of a monitor.
