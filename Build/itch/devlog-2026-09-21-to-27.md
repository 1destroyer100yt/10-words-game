# Devlog: the last week before launch

Jewel of the Devil comes out this Wednesday, September 30. Here is what changed last week. It was a big one.

## Harder

- Two new difficulty levels. Level 4 and 5 make every demon faster, sharper and harder to shake. We beat both, but not on the first try.
- Level 4 and 5 also bring a new demon, the Listener. It is blind. It can't see you at all, but it hears coins, clues and running from much further away than the others.
- Running is loud now. Hold Shift and you get there faster, but any demon close enough hears you coming. A ring shows how far the noise goes.
- The building changes every time you start fresh. The Jewel, the marks and the portal key move to different wings, so you can't just learn the route.
- Marks on the wall don't show on your map until you have actually seen them.

## Fairer

- The first 4 seconds of a run are safe, so you can't get caught before you even know where you are.
- The intro now teaches running, not just walking, hiding and throwing.
- Each difficulty keeps its own best time.
- Winning shows your time, and tells you when you beat your record.

## Settings

- Click the gear on the title screen to change your keys. Click a key, press the new one. It remembers them.
- Three quality levels, for slower computers.

## Looks and sounds

- New title screen.
- The player now floats and bobs while standing still, leans into a run, and has a proper animation for getting caught.
- Every demon has its own voice now. One groans, one whispers, one clicks its bones, and the Listener sniffs the air. When one notices you it snarls or grunts, and whoever catches you screams. You can hear which one is near, and which side it's on, before you ever see it.

## Fixes

We went through every script looking for bugs and fixed the ones we found. Demons don't cut through wall corners anymore. Your best time can't get wiped by the menu. Hiding and getting caught at the same moment works properly. The download is smaller and loads faster.

## Wednesday

That's it until launch. The game still shows only ten words. See you on the 30th.
